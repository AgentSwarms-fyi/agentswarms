# Provider logos

The SVGs here are from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org), released under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): v11.15.0, and
v16.34.0 for `deepseek`, `mistralai`, `elevenlabs` and `vllm`, which v11 does
not have. `openrouter.png` is OpenRouter's mark.

Each logo is a trademark of its owner. It is shown only to identify that
provider's integration, and its use implies no endorsement.

They are served from the app itself rather than from a CDN (R326), so an
install without internet access shows them and no browser asks a third party
for them.
